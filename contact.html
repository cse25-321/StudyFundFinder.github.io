Quick answers to common questions about scholarships and our platform.

Eligibility varies per scholarship. Some are open to Botswana citizens only, while others accept international students. Always check each scholarship's specific requirements on their official website before applying.

We recommend clicking the "Apply" button on each scholarship card to visit the institution's official website, where you'll find the most up-to-date deadline and availability information.

Most scholarships require: certified academic transcripts, a national ID or passport, a personal statement or motivation letter, and proof of admission or acceptance. Some may also ask for financial statements or recommendation letters.
