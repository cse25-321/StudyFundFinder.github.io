About SchoolFundFind

Helping students find scholarships easily.

Who We Are

SchoolFundFind is a platform created to help students discover scholarship opportunities locally and internationally. We bring all information into one place so students can easily access it without the frustration of searching dozens of different websites.

We believe no student should miss out on funding opportunities simply because they didn't know where to look.

Our Vision

To be the leading scholarship discovery platform for African students, connecting them with life-changing educational opportunities at home and abroad.

Our Mission

To support students in achieving their academic goals by providing clear, reliable, and up-to-date scholarship information — completely free of charge.
